# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Glint Library project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission
- Submitting undisclosed security exploits, malicious themes or archives, or unlicensed or unconsented third-party assets and personal files
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

During the current bootstrap phase, repository maintainer `@NeerajCodz` (`https://github.com/NeerajCodz`) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and taking appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, themes, documentation, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Glint Library community spaces, including the `Astraive/glint-library` repository, issues, pull requests, and related interactions, and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainer (`@NeerajCodz`) before posting anything publicly:

- **Private conduct reports:** email [neerajcodz@gmail.com](mailto:neerajcodz@gmail.com). GitHub profiles do not provide private messaging.
- **Undisclosed security vulnerabilities:** do **not** use general conduct channels; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/Astraive/glint-library/security/advisories/new).
- **Main application repository:** conduct incidents in `Astraive/Glint` follow the same standard and are reported to the same maintainer.

Never post private harassment evidence, personal identifiers, or unredacted host paths in a public issue or pull request. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, respecting the privacy and security of the reporter.
